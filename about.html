About Me

Britton Petersen

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Stellenbosch University with a strong focus on cybersecurity, secure software development, and problem solving. I'm driven by curiosity and a desire to understand systems at a deeper level, with a particular interest in building and validating secure, reliable software.

I've gained practical industry experience through internships at MWR CyberSec and Orange Cyberdefense South Africa, where I worked alongside leading professionals and strengthened my skills in Penetration Testing methodology, vulnerability identification, and software validation. These experiences gave me a solid foundation in applying security principles in real-world environments.

I've also competed in high-pressure hackathons, including Standard Bank UniHack 2025 and the SU LaunchLab Hackathon 2025, where my team placed third, demonstrating my ability to collaborate, think critically, and deliver under tight deadlines.

Beyond formal experience, I'm constantly building, learning, and refining my skills. I enjoy working across the stack, exploring new technologies, and helping others understand programming and security concepts. I also spend time customising and optimising my Linux environment, because attention to detail matters, whether in code or configuration.

Outside of tech, I stay active through gym training, soccer, and chess, and enjoy cooking, traveling, and DJ-ing.
