LaunchLab Hackthon Project

LaunchLab

I participated in the SU LaunchLab Hackathon 2025, where my team tackled the Ambient AI Challenge of designing a solution to convert spoken clinical conversations into structured medical notes. We built a full-stack web application that allows doctors to securely manage patients, upload consultation recordings, and generate AI-powered clinical notes by converting speech to text and structuring the output. The platform also included editable notes for accuracy and a virtual assistant, “Dr. Vital,” to surface relevant patient information quickly. Our solution focused on reducing administrative workload and improving efficiency in healthcare, and our team placed third in the competition.
